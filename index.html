Core Requirement Four // Earth Safety Transfer

How Space Fire Data Saves Lives on Earth

Narrow millimeter channels on Earth trap air without buoyancy, behaving identically to Zero Gravity.

1. Electric Vehicle Battery Thermal Runaway (FLEX-2 and CFI-G)

Inside 3-millimeter gaps between lithium battery cells, buoyancy vanishes. Invisible Cool Flames discovered in space explain sudden battery re-ignition and guide safer cell spacing.

2. Fine Water Mist Extinguishers (Fine Water Mist PSI-109)

Ultra-fine 20-micron water droplets developed for Space Station fire suppression now protect hospital intensive care units, server rooms, and museums without water damage.

3. Underground Mining and Submarine Smoke Sensors (SAME and DAFT)

Space smoke particle measurements allow modern detectors to distinguish real sub-micron fire smoke from coarse coal and rock dust, eliminating false alarms.

4. Firefighter Protective Turnout Gear (Saffire I-VI)

Radiative heat transfer models from Saffire fabric burns optimize the micro-air-gap insulation layers inside terrestrial firefighter suits.
