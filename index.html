Real-World Transfer // Earth Safety Impact

How This Virtual Lab Protects Earth

1. Electric Vehicle Battery Channels (FLEX-2 & CFI-G)

Narrow 3-millimeter gaps between lithium cells trap air without buoyancy just like Zero Gravity. Invisible 600-Kelvin Cool Flames discovered in space explain sudden battery thermal runaway.

2. Hospital & Server Fine Water Mist (Mist PSI-109)

Ultra-fine 20-micron water droplets vaporize instantly inside flames, quenching fires in intensive care units and server rooms without water damage.

3. Underground Mining Dust Discrimination (SAME & DAFT)

Separates 0.18-micron combustion smoke from coarse rock dust (>2 microns), preventing false alarms in mines and submarines.

4. Firefighter Protective Turnout Gear (Saffire I-VI)

Calibrates radiative heat transfer across micro-air-gap layers in firefighter suits.
